How to use this estimate

Use measured tokens

Replace profile defaults with tokens from your application logs once traffic is available. Retrieved documents and conversation history usually dominate input cost.

Separate availability from quality

A reliability fallback should normally be a small share of traffic. A sustained high percentage signals that the primary capacity needs attention.

Check residency independently

Pricing does not prove data residency. Confirm the exact model, region, and inference mode with your privacy and cloud teams.
